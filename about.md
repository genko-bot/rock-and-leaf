My journey with tea began at a young age during a trip to San Francisco. I remember vividly how the first sip of a well-brewed cup of tea left a lasting impression on me. It was a simple, yet profound experience, and in that moment, I discovered the magic of tea. Over the years, my relationship with tea has been a bit off and on, but it has always remained a constant in my life.

For a long time, coffee was my morning ritual, providing the jolt I needed to start the day. But eventually, I made the decision to quit coffee and switch to green tea. What started as a healthier choice quickly turned into a renewed love affair with tea. I found myself captivated by the depth of flavors and the nuances in each cup of Japanese green tea.

Now, tea is more than just a drink for me—it's a passion, a way to pause and appreciate the present moment. This blog is my space to share my tea experiences, discoveries, and the ongoing journey of learning and exploring the world of tea.
