## Finally, They're Here!
My seeds arrived while I was on vacation, so as soon as I got back I was eager to get started. As a reminder, I ordered 6 varities: *EV01, BC01, BC03, BC04, BC11, and Keemum*.
Since my seeds had sat a bit, they were covered in white mold. Interestingly, *BC04* had the highest amount of mold. 

To begin, on 3/22/2025 I began soaking my seeds in tap water for 24 hours, as recommended by the University of Hawai'i's <a href="https://www.ctahr.hawaii.edu/oc/freepubs/pdf/scm-17.pdf">Cooperative Extension Service</a>.

The two *BC04* seeds that continued to float after 24 hours were covered in a relatively thick layer of white mold, which could explain why they did not sink.
After soaking, I placed each seed in the seed starting trays under approximately an inch of potting mix. Each tray was labled with their corresponding variety, and the two *BC04* floaters were labled for further observation.
